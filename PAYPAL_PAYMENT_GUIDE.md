# PayPal Payment Guide

## How to Pay with PayPal in the Apartment Management System

### Step 1: Select PayPal as Payment Method
- Go to the **Payment** page
- Select a bill from the list
- Enter the payment amount
- From the **Payment Method** dropdown, select **PayPal**

### Step 2: Start PayPal Payment
- Click the **"Pay with PayPal"** button
- You'll be redirected to PayPal's secure payment page

### Step 3: Authorize Payment
- Log in to your PayPal account (or create one if needed)
- Review the payment details
- Click "Approve" or "Pay" to complete the transaction

### Step 4: Confirmation
- You'll be redirected back to the system
- Your payment will be recorded automatically
- You'll receive a success confirmation

## Supported Currencies
- **PHP (Philippine Peso)** - Local currency
- **USD (US Dollar)** - Automatically converted by PayPal
- All amounts will be converted to USD for PayPal processing

## Troubleshooting

### PayPal button not appearing?
1. Make sure you've selected **PayPal** from the Payment Method dropdown
2. Refresh the page if the button doesn't appear
3. Check that your browser allows JavaScript

### Payment failed?
1. Verify the amount is correct and doesn't exceed the bill amount
2. Check your PayPal account balance
3. Try again or contact support
